What we are designing for

Clarity is a feature.

A

Speak with restraint

Repeat suppression and a configurable pace are core design questions—not finishing touches. Users of the leading app complain it "is continually announcing" and that announcements "cannot be turned off".

B

Make uncertainty audible

A tentative model output should never sound like a certain fact.

C

Keep the user in control

Starting, stopping, hearing a message again, and understanding a failure should be straightforward.

D

Default to local processing

No account, no analytics, no cloud image upload. The app has no internet permission at all, so it cannot send your surroundings anywhere.

Where this stands

We are not
the first.

Google has two apps in this space, and both are better than this one. Lookout works offline and gives direction and distance. Guided Vision in Gemini Live — launched 1 October 2026 — converses with your camera, speaks Hindi, and is integrated into TalkBack itself. Being first is not the claim.

This project is deliberately narrower, and asks one question: can an assistive camera app say less and be more useful because of it? Guided Vision is, by design, a conversation — and a conversation never stops describing. So the trade is live, not theoretical.

What this project offers instead: nothing leaves the phone — no network, no account, no upload of your surroundings — and the measurements that would show whether saying less actually helps. If the answer turns out to be no, that result gets published too.

The research is in the open, including the parts that are still unverified and the open question of whether image-relative position is useful at all.

Important limitations

Helpful context.
Never a safe route.

This prototype may miss objects or describe them incorrectly. Silence does not mean that nothing is there. Image-relative position is not distance, direction in the room, hazard detection, or proof that a path is clear. Do not rely on Disha Voice for independent mobility, street crossing, stairs, collision avoidance, or any safety-critical decision. Continue to use your established mobility aids and practices.

Not a replacement for

  • A white cane
  • A guide dog
  • Mobility training
  • A trusted guide

Privacy, by design intent

Your camera view
should stay yours.

The planned core loop is on-device and session-only. Frames should be processed in memory, not saved or uploaded.

These are product requirements, not verified claims about a released app. The implementation, permissions, dependencies, and network behavior must be audited before the privacy policy can describe them as fact. Optional network features are out of scope for the first version.
