# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Disha Voice is intended to support blind and low-vision people. We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, accessible, and free from harassment, regardless of disability, vision status, age, race, ethnicity, nationality, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, religion, appearance, experience, or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Critique ideas and work, not people.
- Listen to and respect the lived experience of blind and low-vision contributors. Do not speak over them or treat one person's experience as universal.
- Use inclusive, accessible language and consider accessibility in project materials and interactions.
- Be careful not to make unsupported medical, safety, or navigation claims about the project.
- Respect privacy and consent. Do not share private media, personal information, or confidential communications without permission.
- Accept feedback graciously and focus on the project's shared goals.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, insults, personal attacks, unwelcome sexual attention, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information or media without consent are not acceptable. This applies in repository spaces and in public spaces when someone is representing the project or its community.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and project-related events or communications. Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing it. They may remove or edit content, close contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect participants and the project. Enforcement should be proportionate to the conduct and may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer through an established private GitHub channel, if one is available. Do not post personal or sensitive details in a public issue. This repository does not designate an email address or promise a particular response time. If no private channel is available, avoid publicly exposing sensitive details; use repository moderation controls, if available, to flag the content.
